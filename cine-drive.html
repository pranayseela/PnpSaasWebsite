Cine Drive

Professional video recording, built around the iPhone — external SSD workflows for high-bitrate ProRes.

Coming soon to the App Store

The problem

iPhone can capture remarkable footage, but long high-bitrate sessions fill internal storage fast. Creators need a reliable path to record straight to external drives.

The solution

Cine Drive focuses on external SSD recording for iPhone — ProRes to USB-C storage, with a workflow for review and offload.

What it does

  • ProRes capture to external USB-C drives
  • Workflow centered on external SSD recording
  • Media organization and review for creators
  • Built around iOS camera APIs and external hardware
