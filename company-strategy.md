# PnP Technologies LLC - Umbrella Brand Strategy

## 1. Brand Identity
*   **Company:** PnP Technologies LLC
*   **Positioning:** "Privacy-first Apple-native software."
*   **Aesthetic:** Apple-inspired restraint, ultra-minimal, high-trust, professional.
*   **Typography:** SF Pro / Inter (System fonts for native feel).

## 2. Information Architecture
1.  **Navigation:** Minimal (PnP Technologies LLC, Products, Philosophy, Support).
2.  **Hero:** Minimalist company statement.
3.  **App Gallery:** Elegant, consistent cards for multiple products.
4.  **Philosophy:** The "Local-First" and "Thoughtful Craftsmanship" pillars.
5.  **Footer:** Unified legal and support links.

## 3. Product Portfolio
*   **AIExecutiveAssistant:** Private executive memory & continuity intelligence.
*   **Professional External Recording Studio:** External SSD capture & ProRes workflows for iPhone.

## 4. Visual Language
*   **Colors:** Pitch Black, Pure White, Apple Grey (#1D1D1F), Material Translucency.
*   **Interaction:** Subtle fade-ins, gentle hover states, no aggressive motion.
*   **Layout:** Generous whitespace, editorial typography.
