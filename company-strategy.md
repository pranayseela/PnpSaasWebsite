# PnP Technologies LLC - Umbrella Brand Strategy

## 1. Brand Identity

* **Company:** PnP Technologies LLC
* **Site:** https://pnpsaas.com
* **Positioning:** “Privacy-first Apple-native software.”
* **Aesthetic:** Apple-inspired restraint, ultra-minimal, high-trust.
* **Typography:** Inter / system-adjacent UI; editorial serif accents where used.

## 2. Information Architecture

1. **Navigation:** PnP Technologies, Software (products), Philosophy, Privacy, Support.
2. **Hero / home:** Studio statement and product gallery.
3. **Product pages:** Dedicated landings per app.
4. **Philosophy:** Local-first and thoughtful craftsmanship.
5. **Legal:** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service (footer + dedicated pages).
6. **Support:** Email + per-app help; Family Finance Pro / subscription notes.

## 3. Product Portfolio

* **Ai Executive Assistant (iOS):** Private executive memory & continuity intelligence.
* **Cine Drive (iOS):** External SSD capture & ProRes workflows for iPhone.
* **Family Finance Ledger (macOS):** Offline-first private ledger — imports, on-device OCR, budgets, optional private iCloud sync. **Live** on the Mac App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/family-finance-ledger/id6790012195?mt=12. Monetization: Free + Pro via In-App Purchase (subscriptions + lifetime). No bank API / no PnP cloud ledger backend.

## 4. Visual Language

* **Colors:** Pitch Black, Pure White, Apple Grey (#1D1D1F), material translucency, portfolio gradient accents.
* **Interaction:** Subtle fade-ins, gentle hover states.
* **Layout:** Generous whitespace, editorial typography on product stories.

## 5. Trust & Compliance Touchpoints

* Privacy and Terms must stay accurate for App Store listing and in-app links.
* Preferred privacy URL: `https://pnpsaas.com/privacy` (alias: `/privacy-policy` mirrors the same policy content).
* Purchases for Mac App Store apps are processed by Apple; studio never stores card data.
