Private Ledger for macOS

Your financial data
belongs to you.

Family Finance is a premium privacy-first ledger designed exclusively for macOS. Entirely local, offline-first, and secure — with smart imports, on-device OCR, and optional private iCloud sync.

Download on the Mac App Store

macOS Sonoma 14.0+ · Free download · Optional Pro

Privacy & Offline First

Quiet and protected.

No accounts. No tracking. No backend.

Unlike modern finance SaaS, Family Finance keeps your ledger on your Mac. The App Sandbox keeps the app contained; networking is limited to Apple StoreKit, optional CloudKit, and opening Privacy/EULA links — never a Family Finance cloud backend. CSV parsing, PDF statement extraction, and Vision OCR all run locally. Optional sync uses your private iCloud container via CloudKit — we never host or see your data.

Core Principles

The Privacy Guarantee

  • Local storage: SwiftData ledger sandboxed on your Mac.
  • Local OCR: Receipt recognition with Apple Vision — zero uploads.
  • Optional iCloud: Private CloudKit sync across your Macs only.
Product Tour

Built for native depth.

Smart Statement Ingestion

Import bank CSVs with auto-detected separators and saved column mapping presets. Parse Chase Checking and Amex PDF statements with PDFKit — then review, edit, and commit in a duplicate-aware import grid.

Receipt OCR & Itemization

Drop receipt images or scanned PDFs into the Receipts workspace. On-device Vision OCR extracts merchants, line items, and distributes tax and discounts proportionally — with automatic card-suffix or cash account routing.

Rules & Action Items

Priority-ordered category and tag rules match payees and item memos. Seeded grocery and dining keywords help on first launch. An Action Items tab surfaces uncategorized transactions for bulk updates and retroactive rule application.

Budgets, Charts & Backups

Track category limits with rollover, explore spending and net worth in Swift Charts with multi-year time ranges, and export or restore your SQLite database with integrity-checked backups.

Free & Pro

Start free. Unlock Pro when you need it.

Family Finance Ledger downloads free from the Mac App Store. Family Finance Pro unlocks advanced imports, OCR depth, multi-year charts, and more via Apple In-App Purchase — Monthly ($0.99), Yearly ($9.99), or Lifetime ($39.99). Subscriptions may include a 14-day free trial when eligible; cancel anytime in System Settings.

Requires macOS Sonoma 14.0 or later. Built with SwiftUI, SwiftData, PDFKit, Vision, and CloudKit. No third-party analytics. No bank API connections. Your data stays yours.
