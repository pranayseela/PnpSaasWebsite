Family Finance is a premium offline-first financial ledger designed exclusively for macOS. Seamless statement ingestion, local OCR, and automatic tagging rules.

100% Offline Integrity

Quiet and Protected.

No middleman accounts. No subscription lock-in.

Unlike modern SaaS trackers, Family Finance operates completely offline. Using Mac sandbox capabilities, we block all network traffic. Your transaction ledgers, imported bank sheets, and categorized budgets are stored locally on your Apple disk, keeping your sensitive wealth records where they belong: in your control.

Core Principles

The Privacy Guarantee

  • **Local Ingestion:** Process statement sheets on-device.
  • **Local OCR:** Receipt character recognition using native Apple Vision.
  • **iCloud Sync:** Private device-to-device synchronization via CloudKit.
Product Tour

Designed for native depth.

Smart Statement Ingestion

Parse statement files securely. Supports automatic template mapping for Chase and Amex PDFs, customizable CSV column assignment, and local presets mapping to streamline monthly ledger updating.

Vision OCR Scanning

Scan or drop receipt PDFs and images to trigger Apple's Vision framework. The engine extracts merchant data, item names, and proportional tax/discount distributions on-device with zero server uploads.

Rule Prioritization Pipeline

Create automated category and tag mapping rules. Reorder priorities via simple drag-and-drop actions, with retroactive category propagation available instantly upon transaction edits.

Calm Budgeting & Trends

Establish category spending targets with sleek progress indicators. Toggle automatic rollover propagation across months, and explore long-term net worth histories with high-fidelity Swift Charts.
