Family Finance

A private ledger for macOS — local by default, with imports, on-device OCR, budgets, and optional iCloud sync you control.

Download on the Mac App Store

macOS Sonoma 14.0+ · Free download · Optional Pro

The problem

Most finance apps want bank connections, accounts, and a cloud ledger. Households that prefer a private Mac workflow need imports and budgets without handing data to a studio backend.

The solution

Family Finance keeps your ledger on your Mac. Import statements, scan receipts on-device, categorize with rules, and optionally sync through your private iCloud container.

What it does

Statement imports

CSV imports with mapping presets, plus Chase Checking and Amex PDF parsing — review in a duplicate-aware grid before committing.

Receipt OCR

On-device Vision OCR extracts merchants and line items from images and scanned PDFs — no upload to PnP.

Rules & budgets

Category rules, action items for uncategorized rows, budgets with rollover, and Swift Charts.

Backups you control

Export and restore your SQLite database with integrity-checked backups.

Privacy

Your data stays local by default, with optional iCloud synchronization when you choose to enable it.

No bank feeds. No Family Finance cloud backend. Optional sync uses your private CloudKit container. Privacy Policy
