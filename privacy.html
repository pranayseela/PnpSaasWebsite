Privacy Policy

Local-First by Design

AIExecutiveAssistant is built on a local-first architecture. This means your data—your thoughts, your voice, your memories—lives exclusively on your device. We do not use cloud storage, we do not have "accounts," and we do not maintain a central database of your private information.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Because the system operates entirely on-device using the Apple Neural Engine, we have no technical means of accessing your information. Your privacy is protected by the physical isolation of your hardware.

On-Device Intelligence

All semantic indexing and AI processing occur locally. When you "ask your memory," the computation happens on your CPU and GPU. No audio files or transcripts are ever transmitted to our servers for processing.

Purchases

When you purchase a license for AIExecutiveAssistant, the transaction is handled securely by our payment processor, Lemon Squeezy. We receive only the minimum information necessary to fulfill your order and provide support (e.g., your email address for license delivery).

Security

Your data is secured by Apple's native encryption and file system protections. By keeping your data local, we eliminate the risk of large-scale cloud data breaches.
